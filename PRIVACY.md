# Dungeon Dot privacy policy

Published by MrRSMd. Last updated 19 November 2026.

This policy applies to the Dungeon Dot Android closed beta, package com.orsmo.dungeondot, version 8.23.

## Information used by the game

Dungeon Dot runs offline. The game does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. It has no account registration, advertising, analytics, tracking, or in-app purchases. It does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or advertising identifier.

Your saved runs, progress, enemy index, audio preferences, and control settings are stored on your device. Android app backup is disabled. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it deletes this local information. The developer cannot access or recover these saves.

## Google Play and beta testing

Google Play manages installation, testing eligibility, updates, and any diagnostics you choose to share through Google's services under Google's privacy policy. The game does not send gameplay data to the developer. Google Play may show the developer feedback or diagnostic reports you submit through Google Play.

## Contact and feedback

Contact MrRSMd at mrdork29@gmail.com. If you send an email or beta feedback, the developer receives the information you choose to provide and uses it to respond and investigate the reported issue. Email providers and Google Play process those communications under their own policies. You can request deletion of support messages by emailing this address.

## Audience and changes

Dungeon Dot is intended for players aged 13 and older. This policy will be updated before a future release changes how the game handles information.
