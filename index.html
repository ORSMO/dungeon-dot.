Dungeon Dot privacy policy

Published by MrRSMd. Last updated 19 November 2026.

The production game runs offline. It has no account registration, advertising, analytics, tracking, or in-app purchases. It does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or advertising identifier.

Saved runs, progress, the enemy index, audio preferences, and control settings stay on your device. Android app backup is disabled. Clearing app storage or uninstalling deletes this information. The developer cannot access or recover these saves.

The separate Android Preview can request test ads from Google when you choose an ad reward. These require an internet connection. Test Diamonds and simulated purchases are local to the Preview and involve no payment.

Google Play manages installation, testing eligibility, updates, and any diagnostics you choose to share through Google's services under Google's privacy policy. The developer may receive feedback or diagnostic reports you submit through Google Play.

Contact MrRSMd at mrdork29@gmail.com. Information you send in support emails or beta feedback is used to respond and investigate your report. Email providers and Google Play process those communications under their own policies. Email this address to request deletion of support messages.

The game is intended for players aged 13 and older. This policy will be updated before a future release changes how the game handles information.
